# Product Vision

## Why this matters

Dose already communicates with customers across acquisition, onboarding, subscription management, education, support, and retention. The opportunity is to connect those moments into one customer experience instead of making customers reconstruct the relationship across email, SMS, account pages, support, and standalone content.

The app should become the customer's operating system for succeeding with Dose.

## Product promise

**Help me know what to do next, understand my progress, and manage my Dose relationship without friction.**

## Customer jobs

The product should help a customer:

1. Start correctly.
2. Build a sustainable routine.
3. Understand what progress should look like.
4. Recover after missed usage without feeling like they failed.
5. Manage shipment timing before product builds up.
6. Understand upcoming billing before it becomes a surprise.
7. See their loyalty and member benefits.
8. Get relevant education without searching a content library.
9. Add or change products when their needs evolve.
10. Get help when an issue should not be solved by marketing automation.

## Business outcomes

The app should contribute to:

- stronger first and second rebill retention
- lower avoidable cancellation
- lower support friction
- stronger adherence
- higher customer confidence
- better cross-sell and product fit
- higher review and referral participation
- better zero-party data
- a durable first-party customer relationship

## Product principles

### State-driven, not page-driven
The customer experience should change based on customer state, product, tenure, behavior, and risk.

### Resilient adherence
Do not punish missed days. Help customers resume quickly and preserve momentum.

### Proactive subscription fit
Surface cadence, inventory, order timing, and controls before the customer reaches cancellation.

### Progress over content volume
Do not build a giant library and expect the customer to browse it. Surface the right education at the right lifecycle moment.

### One customer identity
The app should sit on top of the existing commerce, subscription, lifecycle, support, and data stack. It should not create a second source of truth.

### Measure business outcomes
Engagement is diagnostic. The product should ultimately be judged on customer success, retention, expansion, and service efficiency.
