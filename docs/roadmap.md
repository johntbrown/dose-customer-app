# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: Product Foundation

Goal: establish the customer experience, lifecycle logic, and prototype shell.

Deliverables:
- product vision
- V1 PRD
- customer state model
- event taxonomy
- architecture
- working Home prototype
- mock customer data

## Phase 2: Habit + Progress Engine

Build:
- daily Dose logging
- weekly consistency
- resilient adherence logic
- progress checkpoints
- customer confidence capture
- personalized next-best-action rules

Key question:
Can the product help customers stay consistent without creating an all-or-nothing streak mentality?

## Phase 3: Subscription Management

Build:
- next charge and shipment
- skip
- pause
- change date
- change frequency
- inventory / cadence check
- future product swap

Key question:
Can we solve subscription-fit problems before they become cancellations?

## Phase 4: Loyalty + Member Benefits

Build:
- milestone rewards
- surprise-and-delight
- redemption state
- review / referral hooks
- personalized member benefits

Key question:
Which rewards improve retention enough to justify their cost?

## Phase 5: Personalized Expansion

Build:
- product recommendations
- second-product education
- goal-based cross-sell
- offer eligibility
- customer-specific merchandising

Key question:
Can product expansion feel like the next logical step in the customer's health journey rather than generic upsell?

## Phase 6: Customer Intelligence

Build:
- richer zero-party data
- review and outcome signals
- support context
- churn-risk overlays
- bloodwork / health-marker concepts where appropriate

Key question:
Can the app become the most useful source of customer context for lifecycle, product, and support teams?

## North Star

The app succeeds when it improves the quality of the customer relationship, not simply app engagement.

Primary long-term measures:
- retained subscription
- successful rebill
- product adherence
- self-service completion
- second-product adoption
- loyalty participation
- reduced preventable support friction
