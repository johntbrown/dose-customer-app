## V0.2 App Mapping

The app preserves the public-site architecture but reorganizes it around a logged-in member context.

### Persistent mobile navigation
1. Today — member home and daily habit
2. Shop — all product destinations
3. Science — formulations, ingredients, clinical studies
4. Rewards — loyalty + Refer & Earn concept
5. More — reviews, FAQs, tracking, contact, store locator, HSA/FSA

### Member-only app surfaces layered onto site architecture
- Progress
- Check-ins
- My Plan / subscription
- Daily adherence
- Personalized recommendations
- Rewards status
- Customer-state personalization

## Product Principle

The app should not become a literal copy of the ecommerce site. The website architecture establishes familiarity and ensures every important Dose destination has a home, while the app adds persistent member utilities that the storefront does not provide.

## V0 Behavior

All major destinations are represented as interactive app areas. Some pages are intentionally demo shells until production content, authentication, Shopify/Skio services, analytics, or other integrations are connected.

## Navigation Rationale

The public site is acquisition-oriented: Shop and Science are prominent. The app is retention-oriented: Today becomes the default destination, while Shop and Science remain immediately accessible. Account management, progress, support, referrals and loyalty are elevated because they are more important after purchase.
