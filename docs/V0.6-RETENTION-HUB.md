# My Dose V0.6 — Retention Hub

## Product decision
The member app should be organized around four recurring customer jobs rather than internal Dose functions:

1. **Understand my product** — what it is, how to take it, ingredients, science and education.
2. **Know what to expect** — set realistic expectations for results and reinforce why consistency matters.
3. **Know where my Dose is** — shipment status, tracking and next-order management.
4. **Discover what else can help me** — relevant product discovery, cross-sell, upsell and bundle opportunities.

## Primary navigation
- Today
- Learn
- Orders
- Discover
- You

Subscription management remains accessible through You / My Plan and contextually from Orders and Discover. This keeps the persistent navigation centered on frequent customer jobs.

## Today hierarchy
1. Personalized journey context
2. Daily Dose check-in
3. Current results-expectation card
4. Continue education
5. Current shipment status
6. Personalized product recommendation

The home screen is intended to answer: **What should I do, know, or expect right now?**

## Results expectations
For the Liver placeholder, the demo uses the current public Dose journey framing:
- Month 1: establish consistent daily intake and build the habit.
- Month 3: many people begin noticing shifts in energy, occasional bloating and overall well-being; the clinical-study timeframe is also relevant here.
- Month 6: useful checkpoint for bloodwork and longer-term progress.
- Month 12: long-term routine / baseline.

Public Dose product copy currently recommends 3–6 months of consistent daily use for best results. Any production implementation should continue to use approved claims/disclosures from the current source of truth.

## Learn
Education is no longer a generic content library. It begins with the customer's owned product and answers the most important post-purchase questions first:
- What is this?
- How do I take it?
- When should I expect results?
- What is inside it?
- How do I store it?
- How do I measure progress?

The TAPP-derived Liver Masterclass remains the sequential learning layer.

## Orders
Orders are promoted to a first-class destination because shipment confidence is a core subscriber job. V0.6 includes a simulated tracking experience with confirmed, shipped, in-transit and delivered states plus contextual subscription management.

## Discover
Product discovery is a dedicated member experience, not a storefront dump. Recommendations should explain **why the product is relevant to this customer** and make adding to the next shipment the lowest-friction commerce action.

V0.6 demonstrates:
- Dose for Cholesterol cross-sell from a Liver customer state
- Liver + Cholesterol bundle merchandising
- Add-to-next-order interaction state

## UX principles
- One dominant job per screen.
- Progressive disclosure instead of dense dashboards.
- Contextual commerce after value/education rather than aggressive selling.
- Product photography should carry visual weight.
- Results expectations should appear throughout the journey, not only in education.
- Order tracking should feel operational and trustworthy.
- Cross-sell should be personalized and explain the relationship to the customer's existing routine.

## Production fast follows
- Shopify customer/order data
- Carrier tracking events
- Skio subscription actions
- Shopify product catalog and cart/add-to-order actions
- Customer-state-driven recommendation rules
- TAPP/education content ingestion
- RudderStack event taxonomy and analytics
- Approved legal/claims copy and footnotes
